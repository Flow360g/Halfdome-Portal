<ShortVersion title="Our acknowledgement">

At Half Dome, we respectfully acknowledge all First Nation Peoples on the beautiful land upon which we work and thrive, the Wurundjeri people of the Kulin nation. We pay our respects to Elders both past and present and stand together with Indigenous Leaders for today and tomorrow, acknowledging this land was, is and always will be Aboriginal land.

</ShortVersion>

## Our commitment

In the spirit of reconciliation, we recognise the continued connection and contribution to nurturing this land, its waterways, and community. We aspire to value the perspectives of the oldest surviving cultures in the world and promise to respect and honour it, striving to learn from and cherish the deep wisdom and enduring traditions of the Aboriginal peoples.

We are committed to fostering a culture of inclusivity, understanding, and mutual respect, in the spirit of the Wurundjeri people. This respect extends to all Indigenous Australians, whose resilience, strength and deep connection to the country inspires us, and provides direction for our own journey.

Just as we seek to unlock potential in the world of media, we strive to embrace the rich history and vibrant culture of the Indigenous peoples, and a vital part of our shared Australian identity. We commit to ongoing education and positive, intentional actions to unlock this shared understanding in society, in support of the Aboriginal peoples. We are immensely privileged to share this land and uphold the values of its traditional custodians.

As we move forward, we will continue to learn, grow and make strides in our efforts to support and champion Indigenous voices within our agency, industry, and community.
