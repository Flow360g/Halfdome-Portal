## What this means for you

| Leave | What you get | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual leave | 4 weeks a year, building up as you work | No leave loading. Plan it with your manager at least four weeks ahead. |
| Advanced leave | More annual leave than you've accrued, after 12 months | Needs your manager and a Partner to sign off. |
| Whole Potential Days | 6 extra paid days a year | Two each trimester, one at a time, no rolling over. |
| Personal and carer's leave | 10 days a year | For when you're sick or caring for family. It carries over year to year. |
| Compassionate leave | Up to 2 paid days each time | When someone close is seriously ill or dies. |
| Community service leave | Time away for jury duty, emergency volunteering and more | Some of it is paid; see the full policy below. |
| Family and domestic violence leave | 10 paid days a year | Confidential. Talk to People and Culture. |
| Long service leave | After 7 years of continuous service | Ask three months ahead. |
| Parental leave | See the parental leave page | Covered by its own policy. |

<Callout tone="important" title="Feeling unwell?">

Tell your manager directly, and by talking to them, as soon as you can. For two days in a row, or a day either side of a public holiday, you may be asked for a medical certificate.

## The full policy

Open any section for the policy wording, summarised from the Leave Policy. Your entitlements also come from the National Employment Standards and Victorian law.

<PolicySection title="Annual leave">

Permanent full-time team members get four weeks of base leave for each year of service. It builds up progressively from your first day, based on your ordinary hours, and carries over from year to year. Part-time team members get it pro rata. Half Dome doesn't pay leave loading.

You and your manager keep an eye on your balance together. If you have more than eight weeks saved up, Half Dome may ask you to take some. When that happens you won't be asked to go below six weeks, the period will be at least a week, and you'll get at least eight weeks' notice.

Half Dome may close the office for a short period, usually over Christmas and New Year, and you'll be expected to use annual leave for those days.

In your first 12 months you may sometimes take more leave than you've accrued, with your manager's approval. If you leave before you've built it up, the advance is deducted from your final pay.

</PolicySection>
<PolicySection title="Advanced leave">

After 12 months with us, you can take more annual leave than you've accrued. It's requested and approved like any other leave, with sign-off from your line manager and a Partner from your department. It's paid as annual leave, so your balance may show as negative. Advanced leave can't be used during a notice period, and any remote working plans need to be agreed alongside it.

</PolicySection>
<PolicySection title="Whole Potential Days">

Each year you get six Whole Potential Days: paid days on top of all other leave, for a long weekend or a shorter week. They're used two per trimester, can't be taken together or joined to other leave, and don't carry over. See [Whole Potential Days](/topics/leave-and-flexibility/whole-potential-days).

</PolicySection>
<PolicySection title="Personal and carer's leave">

Personal leave covers both sick leave and carer's leave. You get 10 days a year (pro rata for part-time team members), building up during the year and carrying over.

Use it when you're ill or injured, for necessary medical appointments, or to care for or support an immediate family or household member. Immediate family means a spouse, parents and grandparents, children and grandchildren, brothers and sisters, and in-laws.

Let your manager know directly and verbally as soon as you can. For two consecutive days, or a day either side of a public holiday, a medical certificate or statutory declaration may be required. Without it, the leave may be treated as annual leave or unpaid leave.

If you're away for a long period you can choose to stretch personal leave at half pay. If it runs out, you can use annual leave, then leave without pay.

</PolicySection>
<PolicySection title="Compassionate leave">

Up to two days of paid leave each time a family or household member dies, or has an illness or injury that threatens their life. The two days don't need to be taken together. Your manager can approve extra unpaid leave.

</PolicySection>
<PolicySection title="Community service leave">

You can take a reasonable amount of time away for eligible community service.

- **Jury duty:** Half Dome tops up the court's jury fee to your normal base pay for your ordinary hours.
- **Emergency volunteering:** if a recognised voluntary organisation is called on to help with fire-fighting or another emergency, you get paid leave for the operation plus one day to recover.
- **Cultural, ceremonial or religious observance:** up to five days of unpaid leave, with supporting evidence.

</PolicySection>
<PolicySection title="Public holidays">

You can ask to work a public holiday and take a different day off instead. You won't get extra pay for working it; you'll get a day off in lieu, ideally within two weeks or on another day of cultural significance to you. Your manager approves both the day you work and the day you take. See [Public holidays and swaps](/topics/leave-and-flexibility/public-holidays).

</PolicySection>
<PolicySection title="Long service leave">

Victoria's Long Service Leave Act 2018 applies. You can take long service leave after seven years of continuous employment. It builds up at one week for every 60 weeks worked, about 0.87 weeks a year. If your employment ends after seven years for any reason, unused long service leave is paid out on your last day.

Request it at least three months ahead. The Head of People and Culture approves it, and a request won't be unreasonably refused.

</PolicySection>
<PolicySection title="Leave without pay">

In special circumstances Half Dome may approve unpaid leave. It's generally not available while you have paid leave you could use instead.

</PolicySection>
<PolicySection title="Family and domestic violence leave">

All team members can take 10 days of paid family and domestic violence leave in each 12-month period, in line with the National Employment Standards. Talk to People and Culture in confidence.

</PolicySection>
