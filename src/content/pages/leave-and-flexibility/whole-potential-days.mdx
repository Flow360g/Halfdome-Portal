## Why we have them

We want everyone at Half Dome to find a balance between work and life, with regular time off to rest and recharge. Whole Potential Days build that in. They're paid days, in addition to all your other leave entitlements.

## How they work

- Use them for a long weekend or a shorter working week.
- You get two each trimester. Unused days don't carry over to the next one.
- They can't be taken together, or joined on to other leave such as annual leave.
- Joining part-way through a trimester? You get a pro-rata share, based on your start date.

## How to book one

<Steps>
<Step title="Talk to your manager">

Check your plans don't clash with anyone else's leave, and think about busy or peak periods for your team and clients.

</Step>
<Step title="Request it in HiBob">

Choose “Whole Potential Days” as the leave type so your manager can approve it. Like other planned leave, aim to have it approved four weeks ahead.

</Step>
<Step title="Add it to your calendar">

Invite anyone it affects. There's no need to include the whole team.

</Step>
</Steps>

## For managers

<Callout title="New starters">

Before you approve a Whole Potential Day for someone in their first trimester, check their pro-rata entitlement with People and Culture.

</Callout>

## Questions people ask

<Faq>
<Question q="Can I take both days together for a four-day weekend?">

No. Whole Potential Days are taken one at a time, which spreads the rest across the trimester.

</Question>
<Question q="Can I add one to my annual leave?">

No. They can't be joined to other leave entitlements, so book them as stand-alone days.

</Question>
<Question q="What happens if I don't use them?">

They lapse at the end of the trimester. Two new days arrive at the start of the next one.

</Question>
<Question q="How much notice do I need to give?">

The Leave Policy asks for planned leave to be approved at least four weeks ahead where you can. For a single day, talk to your manager as early as possible so your team can plan around it.

</Question>
</Faq>
