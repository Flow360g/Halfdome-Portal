<ShortVersion>

- **Talk to your manager first.** Leave is agreed together, with workload and the team in mind.
- Your manager gets **sign-off from your Partner or Head** of department.
- Then **request it in HiBob** with the right dates and type of leave.
- **Plan ahead:** four weeks' notice for planned leave, three months for long service leave.

</ShortVersion>

## Step by step

<Steps>
<Step title="Talk it through with your manager">

Planned leave is agreed between you and your manager, taking into account the team's workload, how the work will be covered and what you need.

</Step>
<Step title="Get sign-off">

Your manager takes the request to your Partner or Head of department for approval.

</Step>
<Step title="Request it in HiBob">

Once it's approved, add a leave request in HiBob for the right dates and the right type of leave. HiBob keeps the official record on your profile.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Checking your balance

Your available annual leave is on your payslip under entitlements, and in your leave balance in HiBob. Managers also see a quarterly leave report for their department, so they can help you plan time off.

## If you're sick or can't come in

- Tell your manager **directly, and by talking to them**, as soon as you can. Give an idea of how long you'll be away if you know.
- For two days in a row, or a day either side of a public holiday, you may be asked for a medical certificate or a statutory declaration.
- Attach it to your leave request in HiBob.

## Christmas and New Year

Half Dome may close the office for a short period, usually over Christmas and New Year. You'll hear about close-down dates well in advance, and you'll be expected to use annual leave for those days.

## Questions people ask

<Faq>
<Question q="Can I take leave before I've built it up?">

In your first 12 months, sometimes. Talk to your manager first. If you leave before you've accrued it, the advance is deducted from your final pay. After 12 months you can use [advanced leave](/topics/leave-and-flexibility/advanced-leave).

</Question>
<Question q="What if I need leave at short notice?">

Talk to your manager straight away. The four-week guide applies to leave you can plan; it doesn't apply to things you couldn't have seen coming.

</Question>
<Question q="Can I take leave during my notice period?">

Advanced leave can't be used during a notice period. For anything else, talk to your manager and People and Culture.

</Question>
</Faq>
