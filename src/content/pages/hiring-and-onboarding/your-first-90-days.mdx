Welcome to the team. We're glad you're here.

You'll find we're a team of smart, caring, driven challengers, and you'll soon see that you are central to our success. This checklist walks you through your first three months, from the week before you start to your probation conversation. Tick things off as you go.

## Your buddy

Before you start, we'll match you with a buddy: a Half Domer in a similar role, usually in a different team. They're not your manager. They're someone to ask the questions you might not want to ask anyone else, from where to get a good coffee to how advanced leave works.

<Timeline>
<TimelineItem when="Before day one" title="An intro email">

Your buddy says hello and lets you know what to expect.

</TimelineItem>
<TimelineItem when="Day one" title="Coffee or lunch">

They meet you in person and take you out.

</TimelineItem>
<TimelineItem when="First month" title="Weekly catch-ups">

A regular check-in, plus Slack or email any time in between.

</TimelineItem>
<TimelineItem when="Months two and three" title="Fortnightly catch-ups">

Until you reach three months. After that, they're still there whenever you need them.

</TimelineItem>

## How we talk to each other

- **Slack** for quick questions and working together in real time. Expect a reply within an hour unless someone's status says otherwise.
- **Email** for anything long, detailed or worth keeping. Expect a reply within a day.
- **A call or a chat in person** for anything sensitive, tricky or emotional, or anything urgent.
- **HiBob** for people news, like birthdays, work anniversaries, promotions and shoutouts.
