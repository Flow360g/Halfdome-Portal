Our values aren't just statements on a wall. They shape how we work day to day, define our culture and guide where we're heading. They're also part of how we grow: our capability frameworks use them to describe what good looks like at every level.

<Values>
<Value name="constantly-curious" statement="Constantly curious.">

There are no guessing games here. Just curious people, with curious minds, asking the right questions so we can dig deeper into our clients' biggest problems and unearth their biggest opportunities.

We're curious about ourselves too. We ask the team for feedback often, and we act on what we hear.

</Value>
<Value name="stay-classy" statement="Stay classy.">

Egos can get messy, so we leave ours at the door. There's only one thing that matters more than our work: respect. That's true for clients, for the work, and for the people we sit alongside every day.

It's simple. Just be kind.

</Value>
<Value name="love-mondays" statement="Love mondays.">

We love coming to work because we actually love what we do. Pair that joy with real ambition and you get some pretty unbelievable results.

</Value>
<Value name="blow-things-up" statement="Blow things up.">

We don't have to do things the way they've always been done. If something doesn't make sense, we blow it up and start again. Because we can.

It's why we've tried bold ideas of our own, like advanced leave and a progressive approach to parental leave.

</Value>
</Values>

## Where they come from

Brothers Joe and Tom Frazer, and their childhood friend Will Harms, started Half Dome in 2017 to unlock the power of marketing to transform businesses. They believed in the power of human potential.

The name comes from the granite rock face in Yosemite. The founders joked about starting a business together while camping beneath it on a road trip, years before the agency existed. It was meant to be a placeholder. It stuck.

<Quote by="Your guide to Half Dome">

We believe in empowering our employees and clients to achieve their Whole Potential, and we are committed to creating a space where challengers thrive.

</Quote>
